### Why not "Free"

Search Console shows hundreds of clicks a year from "free" queries already landing on
the site and finding a paid Replicate flow. Tagging the listing "Free" makes that
mismatch worse and inflates bounce. "Pay-per-use" at $0.03 is a genuinely strong
number — lead with cheap, not free.

## Short description (~160 chars)

Upload a video with no usable audio and get a text transcript read from the
speaker's lip movements. Open source, pay-per-use, about $0.03 a video.

## Medium description (~50 words)

Lip-Reading.com is an AI lip reading tool that transcribes speech from video with no
usable audio by reading the speaker's lip movements. It accepts clips of 2-40 seconds
in MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM, runs on Replicate for about $0.03 a video, and the model is
open source and self-hostable.

## Long description (~200 words)

Lip-Reading.com transcribes speech from silent, muted or noisy video by reading the
speaker's lip movements, using a visual speech recognition model that analyses the
mouth frame by frame. It is built for footage where the audio is missing, inaudible
or unusable.

The model accepts MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM clips between 2 and 40 seconds at up to
1080p, and requires a single face in frame. It runs on Replicate at roughly $0.03 per
video with no subscription, and typically completes in about 30 seconds. The model is
open source, so it can also be pulled and run locally under Docker with no account and
no per-run cost.

Accuracy depends heavily on recording conditions rather than on the tool: lighting,
resolution, camera distance, and whether anything obscures the lips. The site
publishes research-backed guides covering how AI lip reading accuracy compares to
human lip readers, which sounds are inherently ambiguous on the lips, and what the
technology does and does not mean for privacy — each citing primary sources rather
than marketing figures.

## Differentiators (for "what makes it different" fields)

1. **Open source and self-hostable** — the only tool in this category you can run
   locally with no account. Competitors are closed.
2. **Pay-per-use, no subscription** — ~$0.03 a video against competitor bundles.
3. **Sourced accuracy claims** — figures cite the LipNet paper and LRS3 benchmarks,
   not unverifiable "95% accurate" marketing.
